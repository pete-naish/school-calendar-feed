      Help

      Whole School dates are copied from the school's public calendar every six hours, and class reps add class events. It's free to use, and we're only human, so double-check anything important against what the school sends.

      Google Calendar: the new calendars aren't on my phone

      Calendars added on a computer can be hidden on your phone at first. In the Google Calendar app, open the menu (☰) and tick each St Paul's calendar. On Android, if one still doesn't show, go to Settings, tap the calendar and turn on Sync. Using iPhone's own Calendar app with a Google account? Open Google's sync settings and tick the calendars there.

      I tapped my app and nothing happened

      Open this page in your phone's own browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android) and try again. A page opened from inside another app, like WhatsApp, can't always open your calendar app.

      I use a different calendar app

      Copy a calendar's address and paste it wherever your app offers to "subscribe by URL" or "add calendar from internet". Don't open the address itself: that adds a one-off copy that never updates.

        How do I stop or change a calendar?

        Remove it from your calendar app's list of calendars (usually called Unsubscribe, Delete or Remove). To switch class, remove the old one, then tick the new class above and add it.
