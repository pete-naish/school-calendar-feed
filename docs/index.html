St Paul's C of E Primary (Enfield) — Calendar Feeds

Tired of copying dates from newsletters, WhatsApp groups and the school website into your own calendar by hand? Subscribe to your child's class (or the whole school) and new dates — inset days, trips, PE kit reminders — just appear, automatically, alongside the rest of your family's plans. No manual admin, no missed updates.

Apple/Outlook: tap "Subscribe" below (or use the webcal:// link with File → New Calendar Subscription on Mac).
Google Calendar: copy the "ics" link, then go to Other calendars → + → From URL and paste it.
